ParkourFlux

Short stories, narrated, over gameplay.

ParkourFlux publishes original short-form vertical videos. Each one pairs a short piece of written fiction — narrated aloud — with looping gameplay footage and burned-in captions, sized for a phone screen.

  • Vertical 1080×1920
  • 30–60 seconds
  • Burned-in captions
  • Original written fiction

What we publish

Every video follows the same shape: a first-person short story told in tight beats, each landing on a small turn or cliffhanger, read by a synthetic narrator over gameplay footage that keeps the eye busy while the story does the work. Longer pieces are split into numbered parts and released on consecutive days as a short series.

The stories are fiction. They are written for entertainment and are not accounts of real events or real people. No real individuals are named or depicted, and nothing is presented as verified fact.

How the videos get made

ParkourFlux is run by one person. The writing, voicing, assembly and scheduling are handled by an in-house tool built for this channel — it drafts the story, generates the narration, aligns the captions to the audio, renders the video with ffmpeg, and publishes it to the channel's own accounts. Every video is reviewed before release.
